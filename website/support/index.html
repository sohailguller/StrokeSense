Common questions

Is StrokeSense medical advice?

No. StrokeSense provides general education. If you think someone is having a stroke, call 911 or your local emergency number right away.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. Your profile and saved episodes stay on your iPhone.

How do I stop sharing questions with the Stroke Expert?

Open Profile and turn off Share Questions with OpenAI. The suggested questions still work without sharing.
